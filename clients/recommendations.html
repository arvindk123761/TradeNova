HOW THE SIGNAL IS BUILT

Technical analysis & timeframe

These are simple educational indicators applied to TradeNova's simulated chart points—not exchange candles or live market data.

Trend & momentum

Compares the first and latest price in the selected range. A move must cross the range threshold: Day ±0.15%, Week ±0.35%, or Month ±0.75%.

5-point SMA

Simple moving average of the latest five points (or all available points if fewer). Latest price above/below it is one bullish/bearish confirmation.

Price-action confirmation

Counts rising versus falling intervals. A majority in one direction is the second confirmation; momentum and at least one confirmation must agree for Buy or Sell.
